Mobile App Recommendations

Here are a few mobile apps that every student should know how to use for a smooth learning experience.

  • 1. Gdrive

    • It is used for students to share files, photos, and documents easily with other people when they are given access.

  • 2. Google Docs

    • It functions as an app for writing, note-taking, and essay purposes. This is frequently used by students for easy access.

      • 3. Google Meet

    • Used by teachers and students, it is where they can hold an online meeting.
